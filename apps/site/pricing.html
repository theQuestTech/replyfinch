Pricing questions

Why is it free? +

Replyfinch is new. We’d rather have real teams using it and telling us what to improve than charge too early.

Will it always be free? +

We plan to introduce paid plans later. You’ll hear from us well before that happens, and nothing changes on your account without you choosing it.

Are there limits on chats or teammates? +

No. During early access you can have as many chats and teammates as you need.

What about the AI reply suggestions? +

Suggestions from your saved replies are included. Optional AI-written suggestions use an AI service that has its own costs; we’ll explain any pricing before you turn them on.
