About

Customer support that feels human again

We’re building Replyfinch for small teams who want to answer every customer quickly and personally — without paying for or learning a giant enterprise tool.

Most support software is built for call centers. Small businesses end up with tools that are expensive, complicated, and full of features they’ll never use — while the basics, like answering a visitor who’s about to leave your website, take too many clicks.

Replyfinch puts the essentials in one place: live chat for the moment someone needs you, tickets and email for anything that takes longer, and a help center so customers can help themselves. Each part knows about the others, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Replyfinch is made by Quest Tech. We use it ourselves — the chat button on this website is Replyfinch, and the messages you send land in our own Tickets.

What we believe

Simple beats clever

If it needs a manual, we haven’t finished designing it. Replyfinch should make sense the first time you open it.

Fast for everyone

Visitors shouldn’t wait for a chat window to load, and agents shouldn’t wait for a page to refresh. Everything happens live.

Honest by default

Clear pricing, no dark patterns, and a comparison page that admits what we don’t do yet.

Your customers’ trust matters

We keep only what helps you serve your customers, and we explain it in plain words.
