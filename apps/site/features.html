Live chat

Answer the moment someone asks

Chats arrive in a queue with a sound and a desktop alert, so nobody waits. Accept with one click and reply in real time — up to as many chats at once as you set for each person.

  • ✓See a visitor’s message while they type it (you can turn this off)
  • ✓Saved replies with “/” shortcuts and smart suggestions
  • ✓Internal notes only your team sees
  • ✓Transfer a chat to a teammate or a department
  • ✓Start a chat with a visitor yourself
  • ✓Pop a chat into its own window, or work in the side panel

Visitors

See who’s on your website right now

A live list of everyone browsing — where they are, what page they’re on, how long they’ve been there and whether they’ve chatted before.

  • ✓The pages each visitor has looked at, as they move around
  • ✓Country, local time, browser and device
  • ✓Returning visitors recognised, with their past chats
  • ✓Say hello first, before they ask
👀

Chat widget

A chat button that feels like part of your site

One line of code adds it to any website. Match your colors, write your own greeting, and choose which questions to ask before a chat starts.

  • ✓Your color, greeting and corner (left or right)
  • ✓Ask for name, email, phone or department — or don’t
  • ✓Help article search right inside the chat
  • ✓👍 / 👎 ratings and an emailed copy of the chat
  • ✓Works beautifully on phones; pop it out into its own window

Offline messages

Never miss a message

When nobody is online, the chat button turns into a short form. Messages land in your Inbox with a badge, ready to answer by email or turn into a ticket.

  • ✓Answer by email from the Inbox, from your own address
  • ✓Mark messages handled — or turn them into tickets
  • ✓A badge and an alert when a new one arrives
✉️

Tickets

Follow-up that keeps track for you

Turn any chat, offline message or phone call into a ticket. Give it a status, priority and owner, reply by email and keep internal notes — with every change recorded.

  • ✓Open, pending and solved — with views for “assigned to me” and “unassigned”
  • ✓Reply by email; customers’ replies come straight back into the ticket
  • ✓An email address that turns incoming emails into tickets
  • ✓Alerts when a ticket is assigned to you or a customer replies

Help center

Answers customers can find on their own

Write articles in a simple editor, group them into categories and publish them on your own help site. Customers can search them inside the chat window too.

  • ✓Simple formatting: headings, lists, links and images
  • ✓Drafts and publishing; links that keep working
  • ✓See views and how many readers found each article helpful
  • ✓Search inside the chat widget, before customers even ask
📚

Customer profiles

Know every customer

Everyone who has chatted, written or been named gets a profile: their chats, tickets, offline messages, notes, tags and the pages they’ve visited.

  • ✓Search by name, email, phone, notes or tags
  • ✓Edit contact details and notes from any chat
  • ✓Satisfaction and history at a glance
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Reports

See what’s working

Chats per day, missed chats, first reply time, chat length, satisfaction, busiest hours, departments and each agent — for the last 7, 30 or 90 days.

  • ✓Compare with the period before
  • ✓Per-agent numbers for coaching
  • ✓Charts with a table view

Integrations

Connect the apps you already use

Send chats, tickets and messages to other apps the moment they happen — a Google Sheet, Slack, your CRM — or let other apps create tickets in Replyfinch.

  • ✓Webhooks for 7 events, signed and retried automatically
  • ✓Works with Make and Zapier (Webhooks by Zapier) today
  • ✓API keys and an API to create tickets
  • ✓A Replyfinch app for Zapier is on its way
🔌

Team & security

Built for small teams

Invite teammates as agents or admins, set how many chats each can take at once, and remove access instantly when someone leaves.

  • ✓Admins and agents
  • ✓Online / away status for every teammate
  • ✓Signing out everywhere when a password changes
  • ✓Encrypted connections and protection against password guessing
