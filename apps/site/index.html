Questions, answered

Do I need a developer to install it? +

No. You copy one line from Settings → Chat widget and paste it into your website (most website builders have a box for this). That’s it.

What happens when nobody is online? +

The chat button turns into a “leave a message” form. Messages land in your Inbox, and you can answer them by email or turn them into tickets.

Can I answer from my phone? +

The agent app runs in any modern browser. It’s designed for a computer screen; a dedicated phone layout is on our list.

Can emails come from my own address? +

Yes. Replies, chat copies and ticket emails are sent from your own domain, like support@yourcompany.com.

Is it really free? +

Yes, during early access. When paid plans arrive, we’ll let you know well in advance — and nothing changes without you choosing it.
