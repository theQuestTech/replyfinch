Privacy

Last updated October 2026. This page explains, in plain words, what information Replyfinch keeps and why.

Who we are

Replyfinch is made by Quest Tech. If you have a question about your information, use the chat on this site.

If you ask for early access

We keep the name, email, company, website and message you send, to set up your account and talk to you about it. We don’t sell it or use it for anything else.

For businesses using Replyfinch

Businesses use Replyfinch to chat with visitors on their websites. For each business, Replyfinch stores:

  • Conversations, offline messages, tickets and the replies sent to customers.
  • What visitors tell the business: name, email and phone number, if they give them.
  • Basic visit details used to help the business serve the visitor: pages viewed on the website, browser and device type, time zone, and country (worked out from the connection, not stored as an address).
  • Notes, tags and ratings.

The business decides what to collect and controls this information. Visitors with questions about it should contact the business they chatted with.

Typing preview

Businesses can turn on a feature that shows agents a message while the visitor is typing it. This text is only shown live and is not saved unless the visitor sends it.

Cookies and storage

The chat button saves a small random identifier in the visitor’s browser so a chat can continue across pages and visits. Replyfinch doesn’t use advertising cookies or trackers.

Where it’s kept

Information is stored with our hosting providers (Railway for the database and servers, Vercel for the website). Emails are sent through Resend. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the date above.
