Replyfinch vs…

Replyfinch vs LiveChat (Text)

LiveChat (Text): A polished chat tool with AI agents, chatbots and 200+ integrations, paid per user. Its new plans are sold as “Text by LiveChat”, and a public help center is a separate product.

Replyfinch: Replyfinch is free right now and includes tickets, email replies and a help center in the same app.

Replyfinch vs Zendesk

Zendesk: A full customer-service suite for larger teams. Live chat needs Suite Team ($55 per agent a month), and AI suggestions are an add-on.

Replyfinch: Replyfinch is lighter and faster to learn, with a live visitor list and typing preview built in.

Replyfinch vs Intercom

Intercom: An AI-first platform with Fin (charged per resolution), workflows and 450+ integrations, priced per seat.

Replyfinch: Replyfinch keeps it simple: no per-resolution charges, no seat pricing during early access.

Replyfinch vs Tidio

Tidio: Built with online shops in mind, with a free plan and AI chatbots. Pricing is per workspace; typing preview, saved replies and ratings start on Growth.

Replyfinch: Replyfinch includes those on day one, plus a public help center.

Replyfinch vs Crisp

Crisp: A generous free plan and per-workspace pricing. The help center starts on Essentials and the ticket portal on Plus.

Replyfinch: Replyfinch includes tickets with email replies and a help center for everyone.

Replyfinch vs tawk.to

tawk.to: A long-running free option with mobile apps; paid add-ons remove branding or add AI.

Replyfinch: Replyfinch is newer and focused on a clean, simple workspace where chats, tickets and help articles connect.

How to choose a live chat tool

  1. 1

    Follow one real conversation

    From a visitor’s first message to the email follow-up a week later. Count the clicks and the tools you need.

  2. 2

    Price your whole team

    Seats, add-ons, AI charges and the plan you’d really need — not just the starting price.

  3. 3

    Try it with your team

    Put it on your website for a week. The right tool is the one your team actually enjoys using.

Common questions

Is a free plan enough? +

It depends on the limits. Some free plans cap conversations or seats, or hide features like saved replies and ratings. Replyfinch is free with everything included during early access.

What should I compare beyond the price? +

Who the price covers (per person or per workspace), which plan you’d actually need for the features you want, and extra charges for AI or add-ons.

Can I move to Replyfinch from another tool? +

Yes. Add the Replyfinch snippet to your website and remove the old one — it takes a few minutes. Past conversations stay in your old tool.

Why do you show what Replyfinch doesn’t do? +

Because you’ll find out anyway. We’d rather you choose us knowing exactly what you get.
